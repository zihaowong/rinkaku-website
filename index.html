What we use我们的选择

Marine board海洋板

Many thin, even veneer plies, cross-laminated with water-resistant glue.多层薄单板纵横交错，以防水胶高压压合。

  • Best moisture resistance and stability防潮性、稳定性最好
  • Dense core, very strong screw holding板芯密实，握钉力强
  • Higher cost than standard boards价格高于普通板材

Best for: kitchens, bathrooms, balconies适合：厨房、浴室、阳台

OSB欧松板

Large wood strands, oriented in layers and pressed with resin.大片木刨花定向排列，加胶热压成型。

  • Strong and relatively low in formaldehyde强度好，甲醛释放相对较低
  • Rough surface, edges can chip表面粗糙，封边处易崩边
  • Uneven density from strand to strand刨花之间密度不均

Best for: wardrobes, back panels, structure适合：衣柜、背板、结构件

Plywood多层板

Fewer, thicker veneer plies, cross-laminated.层数较少、单层较厚的单板交错压合。

  • Stable, good screw holding稳定，握钉力不错
  • Water resistance depends on the glue grade防水性取决于所用胶水等级
  • Thick plies can hide gaps in the core单层较厚，板芯可能有空隙

Best for: bedroom & living room furniture适合：卧室、客厅家具

Eco board生态板

A core board with a factory-finished melamine face.芯板外贴三聚氰胺饰面，出厂即带饰面。

  • Ready-made finish, many colours, budget friendly免漆，花色多，价格亲民
  • Core quality varies widely by brand板芯质量参差不齐
  • Edges swell if water gets past the banding封边一旦进水容易膨胀

Best for: dry rooms on a budget适合：干燥空间、预算有限

How to spot good marine board如何分辨好的海洋板？

  1. Count the layers. More, thinner plies at the same thickness means a denser, more stable board.数层数：同样厚度下，层数越多、单层越薄，板材越密实稳定。
  2. Check the edge. Plies should be straight and even, with no gaps or voids.看侧边：每层平直均匀，没有空洞和缝隙。
  3. Feel the weight. A good board feels noticeably heavy for its size.掂重量：好板材拿在手里明显更沉。

Is marine board completely waterproof?海洋板是完全防水的吗？

It's highly water-resistant: splashes, steam and humidity won't damage it. Like any board, it shouldn't sit in standing water for long periods — and with an aluminum frame underneath, it never has to.海洋板具有很强的防水性能，溅水、蒸汽和潮湿都不会损坏它。和所有板材一样，不建议长时间浸泡在积水中 —— 而有了铝型材框架托底，柜体本身就不会接触积水。
